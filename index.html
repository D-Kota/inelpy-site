inelpy [in·el·py] n.

1 an independent research venture at the intersection of data science, human language, and machine learning.

Current focus

1.

Lexicontext

A computational linguistics tool in development for conversational AI context awareness. Details will be shared as the work matures.

2.

Language learning, grounded in research

A learning app built on the findings of From Data to Dialogue: Unlocking Language for All — research showing that specialized, corpus-driven word lists give learners meaningfully better vocabulary coverage than general-purpose lists.
